# Brag Plan: SENTRY (PredictIQ) — Transformer Use Case

## What is this app?
SENTRY (PredictIQ) is an enterprise Industrial IoT Predictive Maintenance & Digital Twin Platform that pairs a two-stage deep learning pipeline (PyTorch Autoencoder anomaly reconstruction + GRU sequence Remaining Useful Life regression) with Google Gemini 2.5 Flash GenAI Copilot diagnostics to eliminate catastrophic industrial equipment failures and preserve hundreds of thousands of dollars in capital.

## The angle
Take high-stakes electrical grid infrastructure — a 400MVA 230kV substation power transformer — and walk through a gripping condition-based intervention: detecting micro-arcing and dissolved hydrogen gas spikes at 100Hz before a \$450,000 explosive dielectric failure knocks out power to thousands of homes.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
"A single 230kV transformer failure costs \$450,000 and plunges entire grids into blackout. SENTRY intercepts dielectric breakdown weeks before failure."
Bold Literata serif headline against a technical grid backdrop with high-voltage warning beacons in Terra Calm Sage, Cream, and Crimson.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **100Hz Telemetry Twin:** Real-time multi-channel telemetry cockpit (Top-Oil Temp, Dissolved H2 Gas, Moisture in Oil, Dielectric Breakdown, Core Acoustic Vibration, Bushing Power Factor) with an SVG radial vitality gauge tracking nominal health.
- **Stage 1 Autoencoder 3-Sigma Knee Intercept:** At Day 105, statistical reconstruction MSE inflects past the mathematical 3-sigma knee threshold (0.0082 MSE), catching incipient winding micro-arcing while traditional alarms are still silent.
- **Stage 2 Sequence GRU Remaining Useful Life (RUL):** Recurrent neural network sequence regressor locks onto exactly 34 Days Remaining with a 95% Confidence Interval [31d – 38d], eliminating late-call risk.
- **GenAI Copilot Root Cause & Autonomous Dispatch:** Gemini 2.5 Flash synthesizes thermodynamic and chemical physics into root-cause diagnosis ("Phase B winding micro-arcing"), automatically dispatching Work Order #WO-9402 for scheduled vacuum oil reclaiming.
- **The \$428,000 Capital Preservation Bento:** Transitioning from reactive emergency triage (\$450,000 loss) to scheduled condition maintenance (\$22,000), yielding a 1,945% ROI.

## Outro / punchline
"Zero unplanned grid outages. Predict failure. Preserve capital. SENTRY IQ."
